About Us

At Electech Lab, we believe every child deserves the opportunity to learn coding and develop essential digital skills. Our engaging coding clubs bring programming to life for primary school children, making technology accessible, fun, and inspiring.

We deliver hands-on learning experiences that build confidence, creativity, and problem-solving abilities—preparing students for a digital future.

Our Programs

Electronics

Discover the fundamentals of electronics through hands-on projects. Learn how circuits work, build your own creations, and understand the technology that powers our world.

Games Development

Create your own games from scratch! Learn game design principles, programming logic, and bring your creative ideas to life through interactive game development.

Robotics

Build and program robots to complete challenges and tasks. Develop coding and engineering skills while having fun with robotics technology.
